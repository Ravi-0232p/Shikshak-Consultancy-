How to Use the Whiteboard

  1. Start or join your video class.
  2. Click the Open Live Whiteboard button.
  3. In Excalidraw, choose Share and start a collaboration session.
  4. Copy the collaboration link.
  5. Send that link to your student through Jitsi chat.
  6. Both teacher and student can draw and write together.

The whiteboard opens in a separate browser tab. It is not embedded inside the video classroom.
